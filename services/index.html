Recruitment & Talent Sourcing

A more focused way to find relevant candidates.

Hiring can become time-consuming when teams have to search through large numbers of profiles before finding people who genuinely match the role.

Nova Tech helps businesses narrow that search through structured candidate sourcing and profile screening based on the requirements that matter most.

We help identify suitable profiles and present relevant candidates for the client's own evaluation and hiring process.

From requirement to shortlist

The goal is not to send the largest number of profiles. It is to help clients spend their time reviewing candidates with a clearer connection to the role.

Recruitment FAQ

Hiring support questions

What recruitment support does Nova Tech provide?

Nova Tech can support candidate sourcing, profile research, initial screening, shortlisting, candidate outreach, and early-stage recruitment coordination.

Does Nova Tech make the final hiring decision?

No. The employer remains responsible for interviews, assessments, final selection, employment terms, and hiring decisions.

Can Nova Tech recruit for different types of roles?

Nova Tech can review hiring requirements and determine whether a focused sourcing approach is suitable for the role.

How are candidates selected for a shortlist?

Profiles are reviewed against the role's important requirements, including relevant experience, skills, qualifications where necessary, location, and other role-specific criteria.

Do you guarantee a successful hire?

No recruitment process can responsibly guarantee a hire. Nova Tech focuses on improving the quality and relevance of the candidate search and shortlist.

How do we start?

Share the role, responsibilities, location, required skills, experience, and any important hiring criteria. Nova Tech can then review the requirement and discuss the most appropriate sourcing approach.

Services FAQ

Questions Before We Build

What types of projects does Nova Tech take on?

Nova Tech works on marketing websites, landing pages, e-commerce experiences, UI/UX design, SaaS dashboards, portals, and responsive frontend products.

Can Nova Tech improve an existing website?

Yes. Existing websites can be reviewed and improved through stronger structure, updated UI, clearer content hierarchy, better responsive behaviour, and cleaner frontend implementation.

Can I request design without development?

Yes. Nova Tech can provide UI/UX design, responsive screen designs, user flows, and component guidance without building the complete product.

Can Nova Tech work from an existing design?

Yes. Existing Figma files, wireframes, templates, and reference websites can be developed or refined into a responsive implementation.

How is the right service selected?

The service is selected according to the project's audience, goals, required pages, features, workflows, and level of technical complexity.

How do we start?

Share the project idea, required pages or features, reference websites, existing content, and preferred launch timeline.
